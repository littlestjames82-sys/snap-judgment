The court will now hear your folder.

This is the live demo of Snap Judgment. Pick the folder where your screenshots live and the court issues its verdict on the spot: a tier, the headline evidence, and a share card you can download. No install, no account, no waiting.

HOW THE DEMO READS. The demo reads file names, sizes, dates, and a few header bytes for dimensions. It never displays, uploads, or stores your pictures. Everything happens on this page, on your machine.

Works best in Chrome or Edge, which open a folder picker. In Firefox or Safari the same button opens a folder upload control instead. Same rules, same privacy: the files stay on your machine.

The folder itself, or one level of subfolders, is all the court searches. Pick that folder and the court will reconvene.

METADATA ONLY. Names, sizes, dates, and image header bytes for width and height. The demo never decodes a pixel, never runs OCR, and has nowhere to send anything even if it wanted to. What your screenshots show is your business; how many there are is the court's.
